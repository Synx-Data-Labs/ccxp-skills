## Design

- **TLDR**

  - `epic/scripts/epic.sh` drops its own SUPERVISED/BLOCKED skip verdict.
  - Docs follow: drive, todo, autopilot, epic, ccxp SKILL text and the task template comment.
- **Alternatives rejected**

  - Touching the `holds` store or HUD: out of scope (separate job).
- **Consumer pre-flight gate**: `dev/daily-ccxp.sh` lives in consumer repos, not here; only the docs and template that described it changed here.
